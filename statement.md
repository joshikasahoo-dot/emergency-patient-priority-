## Problem Statement

In an emergency department, patients need to be assessed quickly based on the severity of their condition. Manually checking and prioritizing every patient can take time and may lead to delays.

The Emergency Patient Priority System is a simple Python-based project that helps assign a priority level to patients using basic health information such as heart rate, SpO2 level, temperature, chest pain, and breathing difficulty.

The system calculates a priority score and classifies the patient as HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW priority.

## Scope of the Project

The project is designed as a basic emergency patient assessment and prioritization system. It can be used to demonstrate how programming and simple decision-making algorithms can be applied in healthcare.

The system can:
- Take patient details and vital signs as input.
- Calculate a priority score.
- Identify the severity level of the patient.
- Display reasons for the assigned priority.
- Store patient records for later reference.
- Allow multiple patients to be entered.
- Provide a simple menu-based interface.

This project is intended for educational purposes and is not a replacement for professional medical diagnosis or clinical decision-making.

## Target Users

The system is mainly designed for:
- Students learning Python and Health Informatics.
- Beginners developing healthcare-related programming projects.
- Healthcare technology learners.
- For demonstration of basic patient prioritization concepts.

## High-Level Features

- **Patient Registration:** Enter basic patient information.
- **Vital Sign Input:** Record heart rate, SpO2, and temperature.
- **Symptom Input:** Record chest pain and breathing difficulty.
- **Priority Calculation:** Calculate a score based on the patient's condition.
- **Priority Classification:** Classify patients as HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW priority.
- **Reason Display:** Show the factors that affected the priority.
- **Patient Records:** Save patient information for future reference.
- **Menu System:** Easily add patients, view records, and use emergency functions.
